excerpt: 'The EvalLLM 2025 information extraction challenge, like its 2024 counterpart, aims to evaluate the performance of few-shot approaches for information extraction in French. In this edition, it adds to named entity recognition a very entity-centric form of event extraction. For our participation, we chose to dissociate entity recognition from event extraction. For entities, we adopted an ensemble approach combining two major types of token classification models based on encoders ; for events, we relied on an encoder-decoder model for relation classification. The results on the evaluation test set show strong performance for entities along with a notable negative impact for events of dissociating the extraction of entities and events.'
